# NightGuard Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-05-14
Developer: Qin Fuyao (NightGuard Team)
Contact: NightGuard@vectrakode.com

This Privacy Policy explains how NightGuard (the “App”) collects, uses, stores, and shares your information. We value your privacy and are committed to complying with applicable laws, including GDPR and CCPA.

---

## 1. Introduction
- App Name: NightGuard — Sleep analysis and insights
- Purpose: Help users understand sleep trends and improve sleep quality.
- Commitment: We do not sell personal data, do not show third-party ads, and do not use third-party ad/analytics SDKs.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We only collect data with your permission or when strictly necessary to provide the service.

### 2.1 Data You Provide
- Account: Sign in with Apple identifiers and, if Apple provides them, your name and email address (including private relay email).
- Feedback & Support: Any messages, screenshots, or contact details you send us.
- Subscription: Status of App Store purchases/subscriptions via system APIs.

### 2.2 Data Collected Automatically
- Device & App Info: Device model, OS version, app version, crash logs (if applicable, solely for diagnostics).
- Notification State: Whether you granted notification permission; push token if used.
- Local Caches: Temporary data to improve performance.

### 2.3 Health & Location (With Consent)
- HealthKit: Sleep analysis data (e.g., sleep stages, duration) and related health metrics (e.g., heart rate/HRV/respiratory rate), strictly per your authorization.
- Location: To provide local weather/context; you can change location permissions anytime in iOS settings.

---

## 3. How We Use Information
- Provide & Improve: Sleep insights, personalized suggestions, reminders/notifications.
- Local Processing: Core scoring, charts, trends, and permission status are processed on-device.
- Enhanced AI: When you request enhanced reports, the app sends de-identified sleep duration, stage, and vital summaries only; it does not send raw HealthKit samples or Apple ID identity.
- Support: Respond to your inquiries.
- Compliance & Security: Prevent abuse, maintain integrity.
- Not used for: Advertising, selling data, third-party profiling, or cross-app tracking.

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
- Third-Party Ads/Analytics: **None**. We do not share with advertisers or analytics providers.
- Apple Services: We use HealthKit, Sign in with Apple, WeatherKit, App Store, and StoreKit to deliver app features.
- AI Processing: Enhanced AI endpoints receive only de-identified summaries when you request enhanced analysis.
- Legal: We may disclose if required by law (e.g., court order).

- We do not sell personal data.

We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or data mining; we do not share raw HealthKit samples with third parties. We comply with Apple’s HealthKit and App Store Review Guidelines 5.1.3.

> We comply with Apple’s HealthKit and App Store Review Guidelines 5.1.3.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security
- Where: Authorized HealthKit data remains in Apple Health. NightGuard stores local app records, derived sleep summaries, settings, and temporary caches on this device and in the app group shared with its extension. HealthKit-derived sleep and biometric records are not stored in iCloud/CloudKit by the app.
- Security: HTTPS in transit for optional enhanced AI requests; sensitive local data uses Apple's platform protections.
- Access Control: Least privilege and strictly need-to-know.

---

## 6. Your Rights
- Access/Rectification: View authorized data sources; adjust via iOS Health and system settings.
- Portability: Contact us to request a data export of available data.
- Deletion:
  - Use in-app “Delete Account” to remove local account records, sleep records, analysis caches, and settings from this device;
  - Revoke Health permissions and delete Health data in iOS Settings;
  - Or email us to request export or deletion help.
- Notifications/Location: You can adjust permissions anytime in iOS.

We typically respond to your request within 30 days. Before responding, we may need to reasonably verify your identity using information associated with your account or email to protect your data.

---

## 7. Children’s Privacy
The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. We will delete such data if discovered.

---

## 8. Third-Party Services
- Apple HealthKit (used only with authorization for health/sleep features)
- Sign in with Apple (account sign-in/identity)
- WeatherKit or system weather (for local weather/context)
- App Store / StoreKit (in-app purchases/subscriptions)
- NightGuard enhanced AI endpoints (de-identified summaries only, when requested)

See each service’s own privacy policy/terms for details.

---

## 9. Data Retention
We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described. You may revoke permissions or request deletion anytime; related features may become unavailable.

Example retention periods (adjustable as needed): crash logs ≤ 90 days (diagnostics only); support emails/tickets ≤ 24 months; local caches ≤ 7 days or cleared upon version updates; HealthKit data is retained or deleted by you in Apple Health; NightGuard local records are removed when you delete your account or local data.

Current app configuration (for transparency):
- AI insights cache: 7 days (cleaned every 24 hours)
- Weather background cache: 1 day (cleaned every 1 hour)
- Query cache (incl. temporary sleep data and segments): 5 minutes
- UserDefaults lightweight cache: 30 days (cleaned every 7 days)
- Persisted sleep data JSON: files remain locally, but only entries not expired within 5 minutes are accepted at load time (effective validity 5 minutes)
